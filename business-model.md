# Business Model

## Revenue

- **Freemium:** basic advice is free for all farmers.
- **Pro plan:** 5 DT / month *(estimate, to be tested in the pilot)*.
- **Data subscriptions:** exporters, insurers and banks.
- **Paid reports:** CRDA, NGOs and donors.

## Costs

AI and speech APIs, WhatsApp Business API, servers, two field agents in harvest season.

## Channels

WhatsApp, farmer groups (GDA), olive mills, UTAP, Facebook farmer groups.

## Partners

CRDA, INM (weather), olive mills, exporters, insurers.

## Why data creates scale

The more farmers use Sa9ya, the more valuable the data, which funds more free advice.

- **Exporters:** Tunisia exported 268,600 t of olive oil (3.6 bn DT), 58% to the EU, where buyers increasingly ask for traceability.
- **Insurers:** fewer than 8% of Tunisian farmers are insured. Weather and farm data can support affordable drought insurance.
- **Government & banks:** anonymous water-use maps help protect aquifers; banks can offer green loans for drip irrigation.

## Market

Sfax alone has 396,000 ha of olive trees. Tunisia has about 517,000 farms and 7.1M people reachable on Facebook (2024).
